❓ Help Guide

1
Learn flips flash cards so you can study a few states at a time. Tap a card to reveal the capital!
2
Quiz lets you pick a fun game mode. Answer questions to earn 🪙 coins.
3
Test 📝 is the real challenge — every question is a mix of multiple-choice and typing, and you get a letter grade at the end. Get every question right in a round of 5 or more for a +5 🪙 perfect bonus (paid once for new states).
4
Pick places before you start. Choose a region (the 50 states plus the US territories), a few places, or tap Select All.
5
Spend coins in the Pack Shop to open animal packs 🎁 — some animals are super rare! Collect them all 🐶🦄.
6
Your progress is saved automatically and shown on the chart. Try the Mini Games 🎮 for a few extra coins each day.
7
Tap ⚙️ Settings to change the look, theme, answer buttons, or replay this guide's arrows.
